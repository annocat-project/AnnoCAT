AnnoCAT

A portable, local-first application for annotating, reviewing, and sharing genetic variants. Annotation and result browsing stay on this computer, and AnnoCAT report ZIPs can be opened without installing data sources.

Research use only

Not for use in diagnostic procedures

AnnoCAT is intended solely for research and educational annotation, exploration, filtering, and visualization of genomic variants. It has not received regulatory clearance or approval for diagnostic or clinical use.

Do not use AnnoCAT or its outputs for diagnosis, screening, prognosis, treatment selection, or other patient-care decisions. Results may be incomplete or incorrect, depend on third-party data sources and computational predictions, and must not be treated as validated clinical findings.

Phenotype features use the Human Phenotype Ontology. See the HPO license and attribution terms.
